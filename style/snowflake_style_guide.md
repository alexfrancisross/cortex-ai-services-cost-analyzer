# Snowflake Style Guide

[cite_start]This style guide is based on the "SNOWFLAKE TEMPLATE JANUARY 2025" document[cite: 1, 2, 4].

## I. Creating and Applying the Template

* [cite_start]**Starting a New Deck:** Always start new decks by getting a fresh copy from the Template Gallery to ensure you have the latest updates[cite: 18].
    * [cite_start]In Google Drive, click "New" in the top-left corner[cite: 45].
    * [cite_start]Select "Google Slides" and then "From a template"[cite: 46, 47].
    * [cite_start]Choose the "Snowflake Template January 2025" from the gallery[cite: 48].
    * [cite_start]If you are already in Google Slides, go to `File > New > From template gallery`[cite: 49].
* [cite_start]**Applying to Existing Decks:** To apply the template to an existing deck, import the official theme[cite: 57, 59]. [cite_start]This is also useful for resetting the theme after combining slides from other sources[cite: 59].
    * [cite_start]Open your deck and select all slide thumbnails by holding `shift` and clicking[cite: 60].
    * [cite_start]Open the "Theme" palette and click "Import Theme"[cite: 61, 62].
    * [cite_start]Search for "Import the 2025 Google Slides Theme," select the file, and click "Insert"[cite: 70, 71].
    * [cite_start]Confirm by clicking "Import Theme"[cite: 71].
* [cite_start]**Importing Slides from PowerPoint:** Transferring slides between PowerPoint and Google Slides is often not seamless[cite: 78, 79]. [cite_start]The best method is to start with a fresh Snowflake file and manually copy/paste elements[cite: 79, 80].
    * [cite_start]Alternatively, go to `File > Import Slides`[cite: 81].
    * [cite_start]Click "Upload" and select your PPT file[cite: 82].
    * [cite_start]Select the slides you want to import and make sure to uncheck the "Keep original theme" box[cite: 83, 84].
    * [cite_start]Click "Import Slides"[cite: 85].
* **Copying and Pasting:**
    * [cite_start]When copying slides from an old deck to a new one, select "Match styles in this presentation" to ensure the pasted slides adopt the 2025 template[cite: 89].
    * To copy and paste styles from one object to another, use the keyboard shortcuts:
        * [cite_start]**Mac:** `cmd + opt + C` (copy) and `cmd + opt + V` (paste)[cite: 96, 97].
        * [cite_start]**PC:** `ctrl + alt + C` (copy) and `ctrl + alt + V` (paste)[cite: 99, 100].

## II. Design Principles and Typography

* [cite_start]**Overall Design:** The template is designed to be clean and airy[cite: 103].
    * [cite_start]Aim for a simple, visually engaging story with one key message per slide[cite: 104].
    * [cite_start]Embrace white space, simple design, more imagery, and less text to make content easier to digest[cite: 105].
* **Colors:**
    * [cite_start]Only use the "Theme Colors" and "Medium Gray" from the pulldown palette[cite: 109].
    * **Theme Colors:**
        * [cite_start]Star Blue: #75CDD7 [cite: 110, 111]
        * [cite_start]Valencia Orange: #FF9F36 [cite: 112, 113]
        * [cite_start]Purple Moon: #7254A3 [cite: 114, 115]
        * [cite_start]Firstlight: #D45B90 [cite: 116, 117]
        * [cite_start]Mid-Blue: #11567F [cite: 118, 119]
        * [cite_start]Snowflake Blue: #29B5E8 [cite: 120, 121]
        * [cite_start]Medium Gray: #5B5B5B [cite: 122, 123]
        * [cite_start]Midnight: #000000 [cite: 124, 125]
    * [cite_start]Secondary colors (Star Blue, Valencia Orange, Purple Moon, Firstlight) should be used sparingly, if at all[cite: 128, 129].
* [cite_start]**Typography:** The font family for the template is Arial[cite: 135, 139].
    * **Deck and Chapter Titles:**
        * [cite_start]Use `Arial Bold`, `44pt`, and `ALL CAPS`[cite: 148].
        * [cite_start]Use "colorstack styling" to infuse the brand[cite: 145].
        * [cite_start]On a Snowflake Blue background, use black and white text[cite: 149].
        * [cite_start]On a Mid-blue background, use Snowflake Blue and white text[cite: 150].
        * [cite_start]The word "Snowflake" should be white whenever possible[cite: 147].
        * [cite_start]Keep titles short and use a sub-head for more detail[cite: 144].
    * **Other Text:**
        * [cite_start]Use `Title Case` for all slide titles, capitalizing the first letter of words that are four or more letters long, and also "To" and "In"[cite: 171, 172].
        * [cite_start]Do not use all caps for anything other than deck and chapter titles[cite: 151, 172].
        * [cite_start]Text should always be left-aligned, including slide titles, to help readability[cite: 173].
        * [cite_start]Avoid using bullets when short paragraphs without bullets are practical, as this improves readability[cite: 160].
        * [cite_start]Avoid multiple levels of sub-bullets[cite: 161].
        * [cite_start]Do not underline text unless it is a web link[cite: 162].
* **Accessibility and Contrast:**
    * [cite_start]Use black text on white backgrounds[cite: 186].
    * [cite_start]Use white text on Snowflake Blue, Mid-blue, or black backgrounds[cite: 187].
    * [cite_start]Do not use white text on Star Blue, Valencia Orange, Purple Moon, Firstlight, or Medium Gray backgrounds[cite: 192].
    * [cite_start]Do not use any of the secondary colors for text[cite: 195].
    * [cite_start]When using Snowflake Blue for text, it must be at least `28pt`[cite: 191, 196].
